{# ---------- Features ---------- #}

Everything your workshop needs

Keep the paperwork in one place so you can get on with the cars.

Vehicle records

Add a vehicle with its number plate, make, model and year, and see its full service history across every workshop.

WOF and rego reminders

Track WOF and registration expiry dates and get a clear warning when one is coming up or has already run out.

Easy bookings

Pick a workshop, choose a free one-hour slot and book your vehicle in. You can change or cancel until the booking starts.

Job cards

Mechanics keep track of the work done on each vehicle, so the service history is always up to date.

Parts and stock

Staff can search the parts library and see what each workshop and the central parts warehouse has in stock.

Safe and private

Customers only see their own vehicles and bookings, and every account has to verify its email address.
